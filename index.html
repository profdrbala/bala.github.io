# About me I am currently working as a Lecturer in the Department of Information Technology at the College of Computing and Information Sciences, University of Technology and Applied Sciences, Shinas branch, Sultanate of Oman. Earlier, I worked at Mekelle University in Ethiopia, Kings College of Engineering in India, and Sami Arul College in India. I have 22 years of experience, including 17 years overseas and 9 years after completing my PhD. Manonmaniam Sundaranar University awarded me a Ph.D. in information technology and computer science. Allahabad-Agricultural Deemed University awarded me an M.Tech. (information technology). Bharathidasan University awarded me an M.S. (information technology) and a B.Sc. (computer science). My teaching and research interests include Java/JavaFx/Jakarta Faces, Python, Streamlit, data science, machine learning, cloud computing, and cryptography. I have published over 60 technical research articles, including Scopus, WoS-indexed journals, and IEEE conferences, and I have 6 patents. I am the author of the textbooks An Introduction to Parallel and Distributed Computing through Java, Foundations of Data Science, Cloud Computing Techniques, and Cryptography and Cyber Security. I am involved in extensive and diverse research collaborations with 100 authors across 70 institutions in 11 countries. There are contributors from countries includingIndia, the UK, Iraq, Algeria, the US, Brunei, Malaysia, Saudi Arabia, Vietnam, and the United Arab Emirates to this wide-ranging collaboration.
